    How to read this dashboard

    Terms used

    Daily screen time
    Hours per day the phone display is on.
    App category hours
    Hours per day in social media, productivity, and gaming apps.
    Total app usage
    Hours per day in all apps, as recorded in the source file.
    Very high users
    Users with 10 or more hours of screen time per day.
    Consistency check
    App hours cannot exceed screen time, and the three category hours cannot exceed total app usage. Records that break either rule are marked "Check".

    How the data was prepared

    • Trimmed and standardized text fields, and confirmed 0 missing values, 0 duplicate rows, and 0 duplicate user IDs.
    • Confirmed that ages (18 to 59) and all hour values (0 to 24) are in a valid range.
    • Added age groups, screen time bands, category shares, and each user's top category.
    • Flagged records with impossible hour combinations instead of deleting them, so nothing is lost and you can filter them out with the checkbox above.

    Limits to keep in mind

    • The file has no timestamps, device type, or app names, so it cannot show peak hours or specific apps.
    • Group differences are small. Treat them as hints, not proof.
